# Vertex Industrial Supplies Pvt. Ltd.
## Standard Commercial & Delivery Terms Policy (2026)

### 1. Payment Terms
- **Standard Credit Period:** Net 30 Days from date of invoice for verified credit-approved B2B customers.
- **Advance Payment:** 100% advance or 30% advance with balance against Proforma Invoice prior to dispatch for new customers.
- **Accepted Modes:** RTGS, NEFT, Corporate Netbanking, and UPI for amounts up to INR 100,000.

### 2. Delivery & Freight Policy
- **Standard Delivery:** Ex-works Pune factory warehouse (FOB Pune).
- **Freight Charges:** Free delivery for orders exceeding INR 100,000 in order value within Western India region (Maharashtra, Gujarat). Standard freight charges billed at actuals for all other locations.
- **Standard Dispatch Lead Time:** 5 to 7 business days from receipt of confirmed purchase order (PO) for standard catalogue stock items (IV-200, PV-100, FP-50).

### 3. Tax & Duties
- **GST Rate:** 18% GST applies to all industrial valves and piping hardware goods under HSN Code 8481 / 7307.
- **Quotes Exclude Duties:** Prices listed in approved pricing schedules are exclusive of 18% GST unless explicitly specified as GST Inclusive.

### 4. Quotation Validity
- All formal quotations generated by Vertex Industrial Supplies Pvt. Ltd. remain valid for **30 calendar days** from the date of issue.
